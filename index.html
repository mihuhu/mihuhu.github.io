About Me

About Me

  • Ruihan Hu is a Ph.D. candidate in Cyberspace Security at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), advised by Xi Zhang. She is in an integrated master’s/Ph.D. programme, with graduation expected in June 2027 (tentative). She received her undergraduate degree in Internet of Things Engineering through the BUPT–Queen Mary University of London joint programme, with First Class Honours, in 2022.
  • Her research focuses on data security and privacy in large language models and AI agent security, including privacy auditing, trustworthy data evaluation, distillation detection, post-training safety and agent runtime security.
  • She is a research intern in Tencent’s Hunyuan Foundation LLM Department through the Qingyun Programme, working on trustworthiness and safety evaluation. Previously, she interned at Ant Group and Zhongguancun Laboratory.
