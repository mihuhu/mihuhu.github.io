Ruihan Hu （胡睿涵）

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) (jointly trained with Zhongguancun Laboratory). I am advised by Professor Xi Zhang (and Associate Researcher Jiakai Wang). I am a member of STAIR Lab. My research focuses on Data Security in Large Language Models, including Training-Data Use Auditing, Privacy, and AI Agent Runtime Security. I am currently a Research Intern in Tencent’s Hunyuan Foundation Model Department ( Qingyun Programme ) and previously worked as an Agent Security Research Intern at Ant Group ( Ant Star Plan A Programme ) .
