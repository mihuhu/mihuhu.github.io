Ruihan Hu （胡睿涵）

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) (jointly trained with Zhongguancun Laboratory), advised by Prof. Xi Zhang at STAIR Lab (and Associate Researcher Jiakai Wang). My research focuses on Data Security in Large Language Models, including Training-Data Use Auditing, Privacy, and AI Agent Runtime Security. I am currently a Research Intern in Tencent’s Hunyuan Foundation Model Department ( Qingyun Programme ) and previously worked as an Agent Security Research Intern at Ant Group ( Ant Star Plan A Programme ) .
